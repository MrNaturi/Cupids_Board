Overview

Cupid’s Board is a love-centered digital space designed for emotional expression, memory preservation, and anonymous acts of kindness. The platform allows users to give and receive love in a calm, personal, and visually comforting environment, especially during moments when connection feels distant or absent.



Problem Summary

In a world where meaningful moments are scattered across noisy and performative platforms, many people feel emotionally disconnected, unseen, or unappreciated. Cupid’s Board addresses this by creating a safe, intimate space where users can share memories, encouragement, and love without pressure, judgment, or the need for visibility.



Core Features

* Hero section introducing the purpose and emotional tone of the platform
* Anonymous and private love and encouragement concept
* Call-to-action section inviting users to participate
* Calm, minimal UI focused on warmth and comfort
* Memory and message-centered experience
